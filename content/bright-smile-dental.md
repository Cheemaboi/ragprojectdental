# Bright Smile Dental Knowledge Base

## Clinic overview

Bright Smile Dental is a family dental clinic in Islamabad. We provide general dentistry, orthodontics, teeth whitening, pediatric dentistry, and urgent dental care. Our clinic is at Suite 402, 4th Floor, F-7 Markaz, Jinnah Super Market, Islamabad. The clinic entrance is beside the main parking area, with lift access to the fourth floor.

## Opening hours and visits

We are open Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays. Same-day emergency appointments are offered during opening hours when a clinician is available. Walk-ins are welcome for urgent concerns, but scheduled patients are seen first. For routine visits, booking ahead is recommended.

## General dentistry

General dentistry includes examinations, digital X-rays, scaling and polishing, fillings, and simple extractions. A comprehensive dental examination with consultation costs PKR 2,000. Scaling and polishing starts at PKR 6,500. Tooth-colored composite fillings start at PKR 4,500 per tooth. A simple extraction starts at PKR 5,000, after clinical assessment.

## Orthodontics

Orthodontic consultations include an assessment of alignment and treatment options. The orthodontic consultation costs PKR 2,500. Metal braces start at PKR 95,000. Ceramic braces start at PKR 145,000. Clear aligner treatment starts at PKR 180,000. Final pricing and treatment duration depend on the examination and diagnostic records.

## Teeth whitening

In-clinic professional whitening costs PKR 18,000. A take-home whitening kit with custom trays costs PKR 12,000. Whitening is offered after a dentist confirms that teeth and gums are suitable for the treatment. It is not a substitute for treatment of pain, infection, or cavities.

## Pediatric dentistry

Children's checkups cost PKR 1,500. Pediatric services include preventive examinations, fluoride guidance, sealants, fillings, and behavior-friendly visits. Parents are welcome to stay with their child during the appointment when clinically appropriate.

## Emergency care

We handle dental pain, broken teeth, swelling, and lost fillings during clinic hours. An emergency assessment costs PKR 2,500. If you have facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, seek emergency medical care immediately rather than waiting for a dental appointment.

## Our clinicians

Dr. Ayesha Khan is our orthodontic clinician. She focuses on braces and clear aligner planning for teens and adults. Dr. Omar Siddiqui is our general dentist. He provides restorative care, preventive treatment, and urgent dental assessments. Appointment availability varies by clinician and day.

## Payment and insurance

We accept cash, bank transfer, and major debit and credit cards. We do not bill insurance companies directly. Patients may request an itemized receipt to submit to their insurer for reimbursement, subject to their own plan terms. Treatment plans are discussed before starting non-emergency procedures.

## Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to change or cancel an appointment. Late cancellations may require a new booking deposit for longer appointments, including orthodontic and whitening visits.

## Booking requests

To request an appointment, we need your name, phone number, preferred date and time, and reason for visit. A booking request is confirmed only after the clinic reviews availability. The chat confirmation means the request was received, not that a clinician has guaranteed the requested time.
