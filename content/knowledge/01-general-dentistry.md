# General dentistry

## Examinations and digital radiographs
Bright Smile Dental provides comprehensive dental examinations, including a consultation. Digital X-rays may be used when clinically indicated. A comprehensive examination with consultation costs PKR 2,000.

## Hygiene and gum care
Scaling and polishing starts at PKR 6,500. The clinic can explain the recommended hygiene approach after an examination.

## Fillings and simple extractions
Tooth-colored composite fillings start at PKR 4,500 per tooth. Simple extractions start at PKR 5,000 after clinical assessment. Final treatment recommendations depend on the examination.
