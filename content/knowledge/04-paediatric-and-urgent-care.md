# Paediatric and urgent care

## Children's appointments
Children's checkups cost PKR 1,500. Services include preventive examinations, fluoride guidance, sealants, fillings, and behavior-friendly visits. Parents may stay with their child when clinically appropriate.

## Urgent dental concerns
The clinic handles dental pain, broken teeth, swelling, and lost fillings during clinic hours. An emergency assessment costs PKR 2,500. For facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, seek emergency medical care immediately rather than waiting for a dental appointment.
