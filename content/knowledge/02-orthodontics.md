# Orthodontics

## Orthodontic assessment
Orthodontic consultations assess alignment and treatment options. The consultation costs PKR 2,500. Final pricing and treatment duration depend on the examination and diagnostic records.

## Braces and aligners
Metal braces start at PKR 95,000. Ceramic braces start at PKR 145,000. Clear aligner treatment starts at PKR 180,000. Bright Smile Dental offers braces and clear aligner planning for teens and adults.
