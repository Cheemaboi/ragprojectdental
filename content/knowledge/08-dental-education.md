# Dental care education

## What happens at a dental examination
A dental examination lets the clinician assess teeth, gums, existing restorations, and any concerns raised by the patient. Digital radiographs may be used when clinically indicated. An examination is the right place to discuss symptoms, treatment options, and a care plan; an online assistant cannot diagnose a condition.

## Hygiene visits
Scaling and polishing are professional hygiene services that remove deposits from teeth. A clinician can explain whether a hygiene visit, gum-health assessment, or further care is appropriate after examining the mouth.

## Fillings and restorative care
Tooth-colored composite fillings are used to restore a tooth after assessment. The treatment approach depends on the tooth, the extent of damage, and the clinical examination. Bright Smile Dental discusses treatment plans before non-emergency procedures begin.

## Braces and clear aligners
Orthodontic treatment moves teeth gradually. Braces and clear aligners require an orthodontic assessment and diagnostic records before a clinician can confirm suitability, cost, or treatment duration for an individual patient.

## Whitening safety
Professional whitening is considered only after a dentist confirms that teeth and gums are suitable. Whitening does not treat tooth pain, infection, cavities, or gum disease.

## When to seek urgent help
Dental pain, a broken tooth, swelling, or a lost filling can be discussed with the clinic during opening hours. Facial swelling that affects breathing or swallowing needs immediate emergency medical care, not a routine dental appointment.
