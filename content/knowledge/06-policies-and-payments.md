# Payments, insurance, and cancellations

## Payment and insurance
Bright Smile Dental accepts cash, bank transfer, and major debit and credit cards. The clinic does not bill insurance companies directly. Patients can request an itemized receipt to submit to their insurer, subject to their own plan terms. Treatment plans are discussed before non-emergency procedures begin.

## Changing an appointment
Please give at least 24 hours notice to change or cancel an appointment. Late cancellations may require a new booking deposit for longer appointments, including orthodontic and whitening visits.
