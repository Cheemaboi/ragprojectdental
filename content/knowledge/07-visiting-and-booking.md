# Visiting Bright Smile Dental

## Location and hours
Bright Smile Dental is at Suite 402, 4th Floor, F-7 Markaz, Jinnah Super Market, Islamabad. The entrance is beside the main parking area, with lift access to the fourth floor. The clinic is open Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM and closed Sundays.

## Walk-ins and booking requests
Same-day emergency appointments are offered during opening hours when a clinician is available. Walk-ins are welcome for urgent concerns, but scheduled patients are seen first. Routine visits should be booked ahead. A booking request needs a name, phone number, preferred date and time, and reason for visit. A request is confirmed only after the clinic reviews availability.
